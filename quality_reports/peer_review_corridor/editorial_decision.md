## Essential revisions (all six gate re-review)

### 1. [FATAL — both] The two measurements that carry the title are not yet evidence
**(a) Value-capture number is a calibration with no sensitivity map.** ratio = m·0.787/0.764 = 1.030·m — the headline *is* the chosen parameter ±3%. §5.2 already contains the routes to m≈25% (under-recording 13–21%, "within the range found for transit trade") and m≈34% (censored matched cell) → "a tenth" becomes "a third". Required: (i) sensitivity figure over m∈[0.01,0.49] with 4 anchors (transport-only ~1%, band 6–14%, censored ~34%, full 49%), **stating the m at which "corridor, not factory" stops being right**; (ii) an external anchor for 6–14% that isn't author judgement (BNS wholesale-only rate; regional freight-forwarder / electronics-wholesale margins; KZ BOP c.i.f./f.o.b. wedge for HS 84/85); (iii) one sentence in abstract + §1 that the band width is chosen. **Do not add more I-O machinery.**
**(b) Weight ratio doesn't measure transformation.** median kg_out/kg_in = 0.084 ≈ value flow-through 0.106 — same number; ~9/10 of imported tonnage never leaves for Russia. Required: recompute **per unit** — median[(netWgt_out/qty_out)÷(netWgt_in/qty_in)] across cells sharing a quantity unit, with tier breakdown — or condition on near-pure-transit cells. Explain p75=0.49 and the 11% > 1.05. Since the price wedge is also conceded not distinctive, soften the abstract's "no sign of domestic transformation" and re-derive or drop §2's μ_P ≲ μ_T corollary.

### 2. [FATAL — both] Trim the gate claim to what the case identifies
The case identifies **(a)** the market-access gate as open and **(b)** the institutional gate as *not* the binding constraint for the shock-specific null (QIC pipeline does real work here). It does **not** identify irreversibility as binding — a multiplicative eq. (2) observed only at R≈0 with a sufficient cause (open market access) screens off the rest; the auto comparison varies ρ, I, destination AND local content at once, and local content *is* the market-access gate. Also: §9's logistics-platform opportunities have an *open* irreversibility gate and also go unfunded → points toward the institutional gate. Required (preference order): (i) a within-country contrast holding market access fixed, varying ρ/σ (logistics platform vs surge basket); or (ii) the symmetric press/announcement search for component/electronics/precision assembly 2022–25 (a null strengthens the paper); failing both, (iii) **downgrade throughout** (abstract, §8, §10, §12) to "identifies an open market-access gate and rules out the institutional gate as binding for this null; whether irreversibility independently binds cannot be determined from this case." Option (iii) alone suffices.

### 4. [ADDRESSABLE — both, opposite directions] Re-label ρ → σ and confront Table 1

Evidence genuinely supports high **σ** (EU 11th-package monitoring provisions, compliance risk), and §2 has Ω rising in σ. Required: (i) relabel the mechanism as σ throughout §7 / Table 5 / §8; (ii) state the low-ρ reading is *ex ante* expectation, or give *ex ante* evidence of expected persistence and say the ex post series went the other way; (iii) put the 2022–25 annual series into §7 as a counterpoint; (iv) a paragraph on how §11.1 changes if ρ is high (the conclusion inverts to "market access alone explains the null" — a sharper paper).

### 5. [ADDRESSABLE — A + B's 62 on generalisation] Name the population; run the comparator you have data for

Georgia/Türkiye/UAE/Serbia got the same reallocation with **no** customs union → the framework predicts a supply response, and the trade half is in data already held. Required: (i) named-population paragraph in §9; (ii) a trade-side surge-basket comparison vs ≥1 non-customs-union intermediary (Georgia/Türkiye) with the prediction stated then tested; (iii) two paragraphs on Armenian GFCF (+~3pp, currently a clause) — a construction-vs-tradeable-manufacturing decomposition likely vindicates the paper; (iv) Vietnam/Mexico: numbers with a source + the USMCA RVC citation, or demote to labelled illustration.

## Editor's note on referee convergence

Together: **trim the claims, discipline the prescriptions.** Neither asked for new data.
