## Rubric walk-through (round 3)

### 1. Opens on the puzzle, not background? — PASS (with the same mild quibble)

¶1 (73–79) is the prior — "the textbook expectation is that supply follows: firms expand, new
entrants appear" — set up to be violated. ¶2 (81–90) is the phenomenon. The "missing
investment response" hook itself does not land until Step three (line 113) and the framework
paragraph (line 127). This "prior → violation" structure is legitimate and was accepted in
round 2; the title and abstract both carry the paradox, so a reader is oriented. Not escalated.

### 2. Structure: hook → context → what the paper does → contribution → roadmap, in order? — PASS

- Prior (¶1) → phenomenon + Chupilkin (¶2) → "This paper asks what Kazakhstan did with that
  signal. We proceed in three steps." (line 92) → three `\emph{}` steps (94–125) → framework
  paragraph (127–140) → `\paragraph{Contribution}` i/ii/iii (142–157) → roadmap (159–165).
- Canonical order. The framework paragraph between the steps and the contribution is defensible
  and now short enough not to unbalance §1.

### 3. Literature woven in after the hook, not front-loaded? — PASS

No front-loaded lit-review block. Chupilkin (both cites) in ¶2, after the hook (line 83–86).
de Souza in the framework paragraph (line 134). Dixit–Pindyck, Khanna–Palepu, Rajan–Zingales
in Contribution (ii) (line 150–152). Juhász / Bloom / Juhász-Lane-Rodrik / Gopinath–Neiman in
Contribution (i) (line 148). Positioning against Chupilkin (roundabout/intermediated trade) is
explicit — "which establishes that rerouting happened and its scale" (line 145). Koopman /
Johnson–Noguera (value-added accounting) are cited in §5.2 rather than §1; acceptable, they are
a methods anchor, not positioning.

### 4. Contribution enumerated, and "is intermediation happening" (established) vs "what the host captures + why no investment" (this paper) distinguished? — YES

(i) "Its primary contribution is empirical: to our knowledge it is the first host-economy
incidence analysis of the post-2022 trade rerouting, extending the existing literature---which

organising device rather than an estimated model." (iii) the multi-source deal-data method.
Leads with the empirical result. Round-1 M1 and round-2 residuals resolved.

### 6. Framework introduced clearly enough for §2, and is the multiplicative model over-stated? — MOSTLY

§1 introduces the three gates cleanly: "a framework in which the supply response is the product
of three gates: \emph{irreversibility} … \emph{market access} … and \emph{institutions}"
(line 127–131), each with a one-clause definition, and Contribution (ii) disclaims it as "an
organising device rather than an estimated model" (line 154). In §1 this is fine — "a framework
in which the supply response is the product of" is explicitly analytic framing.

The over-statement risk the brief asks about sits in **§2, not §1**: line 263 "The three gates
combine multiplicatively, **so** the investment response to the shock is [eq. \eqref{eq:gates}]"
and line 272 "The structure is multiplicative: a closed gate anywhere drives $R$ to zero." The
"so" plus a display equation with a label reads as a derived structural result; it is a
stipulation (a closed gate ⇒ R = 0 is definitional to the "gate" metaphor). The §2 mediation
paragraph does disclaim ("an interpretive device, not … a model we estimate", line 281–282),
but two paragraphs later. This is a Lens 3 concern primarily; from the intro's side, §1 is
clean because (ii)'s disclaimer is close and unambiguous. Flagged MINOR (L3-6) for the record.

### 7. Neutral framing — PASS, clean

§1 contains no "circumvention," "evasion," "sanctions-busting," or "busting." The vocabulary is
"redirection of trade through third countries" (line 83), "onward movement to Russia" (line 86),
"reorientation" throughout, "the outflow to Russia." The abstract is equally clean ("part of it
was rerouted through neighbouring economies", line 38–39). The one place the regulatory term
appears — §7 line 745, "the EU's eleventh sanctions package … which introduced an
anti-circumvention transit tool" — is naming the instrument, not characterising Kazakhstan, and
is outside §1. No finding.
