===== RQ2(b): domestic value capture from the trade reorientation =====

baseline 2018-21 : KZ->Russia $11 m/yr | Western inbound $169 m/yr | West+China inbound $444 m/yr
incremental 2022-25 (reorientation-attributable): outbound $479 m ; Western inbound $887 m ; West+China inbound $3158 m
incremental flow-through: out / Western in = 0.54 ;  out / West+China in = 0.15

matched-cell unit-value wedge (07), descriptive only: censored gross margin = +0.34 ; value-weighted aggregate = -0.49
  -> not a usable margin estimate (censoring inflates; the aggregate is a c.i.f./f.o.b. + under-invoicing artifact).
  -> m taken instead from the KZ national-accounts trade+insurance+freight convention: 6-14%.

HEADLINE: of ~$479 m in incremental exports to Russia after 2022, Kazakhstan retains
~$23-53 m as domestic value added, i.e. 5-11% of the gross flow (midpoint ~8%),
against ~76% per dollar of domestic manufacturing output --- a rerouted dollar creates on
the order of a tenth as much domestic value as a produced one. Corridor, not factory.

CAVEAT: single I-O vintage (OECD ICIO 2019); the ICIO's KZ domestic-content shares are
relatively high, so v_TT and v_M are likely upper bounds. An ADB MRIO / KZ BNS 68-product
cross-check is left for a robustness appendix. (Fiscal is 09_*.R.)

-- #7: counterfactual sensitivity of the incremental outbound flow --
   pre-2022 outbound $m/yr : 17.4, 12.2, 7.1, 8.4  (declining)
   flat cf (2018-21 mean)  : 11.3/yr  -> increment = $479 m  (headline)
   linear-trend cf         : 3.2, 0, 0, 0      -> increment = $521 m
   => the flat counterfactual is conservative; the trend cf raises the increment ~9%

-- #8: decomposition of the incremental Western inbound ($887m) --
   onward to Russia (KZ-reported exports)      : $479 m  (54%)
   onward to non-Russia destinations           : $41 m  (5%)
   residual (domestic absorption + inventory + c.i.f./mirror measurement gap): $367 m  (41%)
   => onward diversion to third countries is small; the gap is domestic use + measurement,
      not hidden re-export.
