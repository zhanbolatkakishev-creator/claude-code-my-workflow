HEADLINE: of ~$479 m in incremental exports to Russia after 2022, Kazakhstan retains
~$23-45 m as domestic value added, i.e. 5-9% of the gross flow (midpoint ~8%),
against ~76% per dollar of domestic manufacturing output. A rerouted dollar creates on the
order of a tenth to a fifth as much domestic value as a produced one. Corridor, not factory.
