===== C3: how precise is the "no investment response" null? =====

pre  2016-2021 : n years = 7, counts = 0,6,15,10,8,6,6, mean = 7.29/yr, sd = 4.57
post 2022-2025 : n years = 4, counts = 3,12,9,6, mean = 7.50/yr, sd = 3.87
post 2023-2025 : n years = 3, counts = 12,9,6, mean = 9.00/yr (drops shock year 2022)

Welch t-test post vs pre : diff = +0.21/yr, 95% CI [-5.86, +6.29], p = 0.94
Welch t-test 2023-25 vs pre : diff = +1.71/yr, 95% CI [-4.28, +7.70], p = 0.51

Poisson rate ratio (post/pre) = 1.03, 95% CI [0.63, 1.65], p = 0.91
Poisson rate ratio (2023-25/pre) = 1.24, 95% CI [0.74, 2.01], p = 0.39

MDE (80% power, alpha .05, Poisson, lambda0 = 7.29, n_post = 4):
   smallest detectable rate ratio ~ 1.8x  (i.e. we could only reliably detect
   an increase to >= 13.1 deals/yr; a doubling to 14.6 is at ~94% power)

Read: the data can rule out a LARGE surge in dealmaking, not a modest one. The claim is
'no detectable step-up', with the detectable threshold around a doubling.
