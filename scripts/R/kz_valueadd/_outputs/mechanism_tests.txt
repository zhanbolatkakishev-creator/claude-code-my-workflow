Public record (triangulation): post-2022 KZ launched/announced Chinese-brand SKD plants 
(Changan/Haval/Chery ~90k units), a KIA plant ($200m), Skoda assembly, a car-multimedia 
plant (Almaty, 2024). NOTE: auto-sector DEAL counts actually fell after 2022 (6 -> 3); the 
capacity evidence is press/announcement triangulation, not deal data, and no symmetric 
announcement search was run for components. Much of the auto capacity also predates 2022.

INTERPRETATION (illustrative, not a controlled test): the durable auto shock coincided with 
new assembly capacity while the transitory component shock did not, which is SUGGESTIVE that a 
durable, transformation-requiring shock draws capacity where a transitory re-export shock does 
not. But the two shocks differ in sunk-cost intensity, in whether market access requires domestic 
content (EAEU rules bind for vehicles, not the re-export), and in destination market -- so this 
is not a clean rho experiment. See corridor.tex Section 7.
