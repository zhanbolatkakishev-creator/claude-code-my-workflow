\begin{itemize}
\item Trade through Kazakhstan to Russia rose roughly tenfold after February 2022.
\item A rerouted dollar creates about a tenth the value added of a produced one.
\item Three commercial deal databases show no investment response to the shock.
\item A customs union makes trade a close substitute for domestic production.
\item Kazakhstan's least capital-constrained investor also did not build capacity.
