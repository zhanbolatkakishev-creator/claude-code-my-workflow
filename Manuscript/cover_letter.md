# Cover Letter — "Corridor, Not Factory"

**To the Editor, Journal of International Economics**

Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit my manuscript, "Corridor, Not Factory: Trade Reorientation and the Missing Investment Response in Kazakhstan, 2022–2025," for consideration as a research article in the *Journal of International Economics*.

The paper studies a large, sector-specific trade reorientation through Kazakhstan following the disruption of Russia's direct trade with its main partners after February 2022, measures what Kazakhstan retains in domestic value added from the reoriented flow, and tests whether the shock induced domestic investment using deal-level data from three commercial databases. It finds no detectable investment response and interprets the null through a three-gate framework — market access, irreversibility, and capital-market institutions — arguing that Kazakhstan's customs-union membership with Russia makes trade a close substitute for domestic production, so the corridor absorbs the shock without industrializing.

**Data availability and replication.** All trade, macroeconomic, and input–output data used are public and either shipped in derived form or regenerable from the accompanying code. The deal-level data underlying the investment-response analysis (Section 7, Table 3) are licensed from Capital IQ (S&P), PitchBook, and Preqin under academic terms that prohibit redistribution of deal-level records. **I therefore request an exemption from full data deposit for this component under the journal's replication policy.** In its place, the replication package provides (i) the complete query specification for all three databases (geography filter, deal types, date range, industry taxonomy, extraction date; Appendix A), (ii) the native source deal identifiers for every record in the consolidated universe, and (iii) the full reconciliation and classification code, so that a researcher with the same academic subscriptions can reconstruct an identical extract. Only aggregate, non-deal-level counts (Table 3) are shipped in the public deposit. All other data and full code are provided and will be deposited via Mendeley Data prior to acceptance, per journal policy.

**Prior review.** This manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere and is not under consideration at any other journal.

**Declaration of interest.** I am employed by the Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC), which is institutionally adjacent to Kazakhstan's state investment vehicles discussed in the paper; full disclosure is in the manuscript's Declaration of Competing Interest section, and I will also complete the journal's separate Declaration of Interest form.

I believe this paper will be of interest to *JIE* readers working on trade reorientation, sanctions and third-country intermediation, investment under uncertainty, and industrial policy in transit and transition economies.
